# Zo's Ark

An exploration adventure about discovering wildlife and building a living animal journal. Walk through an Ark sanctuary campus and outdoor habitats, sail into the surrounding sea, rescue animals with nonlethal gear, and trade them into the sanctuary to fund the next expedition.

## First playable

The hybrid world adapts all three user-supplied floor plans. The original Ark retains its southern lobby, central avenue, courtyards, habitat halls, pointed Atrium and eastern marine hall. Two additional islands, Canopy Reach and Moonstone Isle, surround the eastern sea, with west-facing landings and free boats. Thirty species span six rarity tiers, including three night-only predators. Rarity, rescue difficulty, habitat equipment, and place in the food chain determine their value and required gear.

The player starts with 100 coins, Tier 1 rescue gear, and 24 inventory spaces. Catching an animal earns Ark Points and a small coin stipend. Sanctuary trading pays its full listed value and frees a slot. Discoveries remain in the journal after trading. Gear upgrades are permanent and apply automatically; calming bait is consumed on each eligible attempt, including failures. Animal capture is nonlethal; there is no player combat.

Coins and Ark Points can both buy store items. Spending points can reduce the current level; Ark Points determine level `1 + floor(sqrt(points / 120))`; surprise chest traps can reduce points but never below zero. Distance rewards arrive every 500 plausible horizontal studs. Daily challenges reset at midnight UTC and require an explicit reward claim. Aurora Tide runs for eight minutes every thirty minutes, introduces the Aurora Manta in a secluded coral alcove, and awards coins, points, and a permanent title for three event catches. Each player may rescue three mantas per tide. Studio previews the event by default.

Capture holds scale from 0.65 to 1.2 seconds with rarity, with a 2.25-second server cooldown and verified hold timing. Common/Uncommon animals can choose rooms on their own island and follow Roblox PathfindingService routes, with no jumping and water excluded. Every movement segment is swept against walls. Only two path computations run concurrently; failed routes leave animals in place until a later attempt. Secluded habitats and marine animals retain local roaming. Rare/Epic/Legendary species can spawn in public and hidden habitats on all islands and sea routes. They stay near their spawn habitat; paid islands offer higher probabilities and hidden habitats multiply them by 1.8. Mythic mantas remain event-only.

Seven checkpoints save the active respawn; each pays 15 coins/10 points on first activation only. The four expedition goals cover Canopy's three checkpoints and four rescues, Moonstone's three checkpoints plus three rescues including a Rare+, three-island travel, and opening three distinct chests. Expedition rewards are claimed once and persist independently of daily challenges.

Twelve free treasure finds (eight chests and four wrapped gifts) roll on the server. Each player can open each stable ID once per UTC day; the opening is reserved before applying its result, including traps. Default outcomes are coins (35%), an animal (30%), equipment/cosmetics (16%), a sprint boost (2.8%), a gentle snare (1.2%), a point penalty (10%), or character death (5%). Obscure finds may contain Rare+ animals; ordinary finds may not. Event animals never appear in chests. Full-pack animal prizes convert to their value in coins plus discovery points; chest animals do not count as expedition rescues. Point penalties remove 12% of current points, at least 10 when possible. Death traps respawn at the active checkpoint and retain inventory and equipment. Prompts disclose the trap risk.

LootLocations samples a random initial room and tries relocating each find after 300 seconds, or 12 seconds after opening. A player within 70 studs or an unfinished world lid animation delays relocation. Destination checks preserve the island and hidden/marine classification, reject paving, occupied space and key approaches, and fall back to the current position if no safe sample succeeds. Claim IDs remain stable across movement; each client marks its own finds OPENED TODAY until the UTC rollover. Chests and gifts use the same free outcome rules and rarity-scaled cutscene, with gift wrapping in the gift scene.

EncounterLocations adds unused meadow/jungle/summit rooms as possible spawn homes without adding population slots. Each respawn samples matching homes and avoids the prior position and overlapping wildlife. Origin slots retain replenishment counts, rarity budgets, map rights and per-night predator quotas even when the actual home changes. Island, biome, obscure/public and seaway restrictions remain; event mantas stay at their event site. Living animals keep navigated movement rather than teleporting, with 3–7-second pauses between calm moves.

Walking pays 2 coins and 1 point every ten estimated steps (30 studs); swimming pays 3 coins and 2 points every ten estimated strokes (40 studs). The server samples physical displacement, Terrain water, grounding and seat state. Boats, airtime, stationary jitter and implausible speed do not qualify. Walking and swimming have separate cumulative distances, so fractional progress survives reconnects without repeating a paid boundary. These are plausibility checks, not comprehensive movement anti-cheat.

Water exploration uses a session-only fatigue reserve: 30 seconds without gear, 60 with a snorkel, or 180 with the diving kit. A life jacket adds 60 seconds. Swimming drains the reserve even while idle; exhaustion deals 10 health per second after the reserve reaches zero. Land and boat rest recover 8 seconds per second. Life-ring pickups restore 15 seconds and suspend fatigue for 20 seconds; first-aid floats restore 25 health and 20 seconds of endurance. Server-validated shared pickups respawn after 90 seconds at a randomized nearby water position. Reserves reset on character respawn, while permanent equipment and earned progression remain in the journal.

## Map integration

MAP 1.jpeg was supplied as an inline floor-plan image. Per the user's preference, the default world mixes architectural circulation with outdoor biomes and waterways. The layout is a hand-traced gameplay interpretation: main shapes and room groups are retained, smaller partitions consolidated, roofs omitted, and an open-sea area added. See [MAP1_DESIGN.md](MAP1_DESIGN.md) and the generated diagram. A Studio `ZoMap` can still override the generated campus; see [MAP_IMPORT.md](MAP_IMPORT.md).

Dry foundation tiles extend below the seabed, preventing an underwater route beneath the campus. The pools and sea gate remain cut out. Four outer shore stairs provide water exits without changing the main plan. Animal blueprints use original ellipsoids, blocks and wedges for species-specific anatomy and markings; server animation supplies walking, flipper, wing and tail motion. They remain stylized Roblox models, with lighting and performance still requiring Studio review.

## Trust and saving

Only the server picks species, rolls captures, moves wildlife, checks distance, prices trades, buys equipment, and awards challenges. The client asks for named actions and renders server snapshots. Capture prompts are rechecked for proximity and cooldown. Purchases and trades require the Ark station. Profiles use a versioned DataStore record with a renewable session lease; load failures never overwrite existing player data. Studio uses temporary session data unless explicitly configured otherwise.

This is a playable foundation with procedural placeholder art. It is not a launch certification. Real-client playtests, multiplayer exploit testing, persistence/reconnect checks, imported-map traversal, mobile/controller usability, and performance must pass before publication.

## Outfitters release

The physical shop occupies a 38×24-stud footprint within the existing Ark Atrium and keeps its north entrance/checkpoint approach clear. Original merchandise props appear on shelves and in Store viewport cards. Fifteen items accept alternate coin/point prices. Field Kit supplies and cosmetic slots are server-validated and saved additively in schema v1.

All species flee on a failed eligible capture; twelve skittish species also flee on close approach. Four-second bursts repeatedly steer away from the pursuer, with safe sidesteps and bounded pathfinding around corners. Player characters are excluded from wildlife collision probes, so standing on an animal cannot pin it. Birds take a bounded flight arc and land. Snared animals remain held until the snare expires. Camouflage shortens alert radius; a 45-second sprint aids pursuit. Gentle snares expire after 45 seconds or hold one land animal for 8 seconds. Fleeing changes capture chance by -0.14, snaring by +0.22, with existing clamps and tier/gear/capacity gates retained. Scanner equipment reveals up to six signals within 140 studs for 20 seconds. Rare and secluded wildlife retains local movement rather than traveling between rooms.

Chests use a fullscreen 3D ViewportFrame cutscene with its own animated camera, shaking chest, hinged lid and a model of the server-selected reward. Presentation strength increases from coin confetti to balloons and multiple fireworks for high rarities. Pure ChestSceneRules bounds scene durations and geometry budgets. Only one scene runs at a time; replacing it cleans up the old one. Skip, completion, respawn, ordinary damage and errors restore controls and HUD; an independent deadline also ends playback. Trap reveals use a separate danger visual. Skipping changes no reward: the server has already reserved and granted the opening.

Robux products grant guaranteed sprint packs, snare packs, cosmetics, or a ranger equipment bundle. Prices come from Roblox; unconfigured offers are marked Coming soon. The server grants through ProcessReceipt, stores an idempotency marker with the reward, and acknowledges only after successful persistence. No client purchase-finished event grants items.

## Island passports and optional teleportation

Ark access and shared sea routes are free. Permanent Canopy/Moonstone entry costs 1,200/2,600 coins or 800/1,700 points. New islands must register prices, rarity budgets and a landing in Catalog.Maps; additional world plans fail startup if unregistered or free. Purchase rights are additive v1 journal data. Prior checkpoint visits remain history and do not unlock a newly paid island.

Travel offers optional one-way landing teleports for coins, points or a pass. Paid teleports never bypass map access. Physical entry/captures/chests/checkpoints and respawns also enforce ownership. Shared passengers lacking a destination pass are unseated and returned to the Ark; an owner's boat remains intact. Accepted teleports clear movement samples and velocity; failed moves refund their own debit.

Five sea routes add 15 population slots and Legendary Sunburst Rays expand reef encounters. See the pricing, rarity budgets and safeguards in README. A fixed screen wallet animates authoritative balance changes. Store shelf models/tags and viewport previews select a product before its coin/point purchase. Colors.luau supplies the brighter world/UI palette. Store inspection is a client request validated for shop proximity and visibility on the server. NotebookState invalidates pending inspections when the panel closes or a chest scene starts; dismissing with ×, Escape, controller Back or the backdrop never waits for a server reply.

## Personal background music

Ark Radio offers Lofi, Hip Hop, Pop, Club, Trap and Ambient playlists. Each player's local SoundGroup has its own on/off and volume settings, starting with Lofi at 25%. The Music page (N) provides title/artist credits, playback progress, track selection and previous/next controls; the tide banner has a quick mute button. On narrow screens the eight navigation buttons use two rows. Settings persist through respawns in the same session, without changing schema v1.

The server queries Roblox's music library for fixed genre terms, Music subtype and 60–360-second durations. MusicLibrary caches at most twelve normalized tracks per genre, accepts optional curated replacements, spaces searches by eight seconds and holds failed searches for 45 seconds. A dedicated read-only remote with per-player limits keeps music requests independent of gameplay transactions. Clients skip failed audio, stop after exhausting a playlist and reject stale replies after mute or genre changes. Chest scenes lower music to one quarter of the selected volume and restore it without unmuting an off player. Actual audio-library searches, listening and device input still need Studio testing.

## Outdoor forestry

ForestryLayout deterministically places varied groves on the existing three island footprints: broadleaf/birch trees, mountain evergreens and tropical palms, with ferns, bright flowers, mossy stones and fallen logs. The default generated totals are 421 trees and 540 undergrowth props. Each plan is capped at 160 trees and 180 ground props, with fourteen-stud minimum trunk spacing. No source-image geometry, animals, marker IDs, access rights or saved journals change.

Plant positions avoid water, ordinary indoor rooms, paving, walls, dry-ground edges, dock/shore approaches and key markers. Habitat centers and rectangular habitat-door approaches retain clearings. Original sparse corner trees/rocks are replaced by these groves; new-island meadow/jungle floors use grass. Forestry models are anchored, noncolliding, nontouchable and nonqueryable scenery, with individual atomic streaming and no per-frame updates. This preserves wildlife/capture/navigation checks while adding shade and visual cover. Tree rendering, camera sightlines and real-device performance require the Studio checklist.

## Night expeditions

DayNight owns an authoritative 1,500-second loop: 1,200 seconds (20 minutes) from 06:00 to 18:00, then 300 seconds (5 minutes) back to 06:00. Fresh servers begin at dawn for a full first day. Server lighting interpolates brightness/ambient color; clients derive the clock from the server epoch. Studio may set ZoPreviewNight=true before Play to start halfway through the configured night; published servers ignore it. Aurora Tide remains independent.

Night capture coin stipends and discovery points multiply by two; dangerous night species use a total multiplier of three. Inventory, daily/expedition progress and tide quotas increment once. Trades/chests/challenges do not receive the capture multiplier. Rewards use server cycle/restraint state; clients cannot supply either. Existing tranquilizers gain a 35-stud nearest-visible-predator action that sedates for 45 seconds and cools for 12 seconds. Reinforced ground snares wait for 180 seconds and hold one predator for 180 seconds, ending earlier if captured or at dawn. Normal gentle snares retain their eight-second hold and cannot restrain predators. Tier, habitat access and pack gates remain intact.

Nine map-qualified NightHabitats entries support one boar, panther and shark per island per night, tracked by origin slot and cycle ID even if a new compatible home is chosen. They are excluded from ordinary spawns/chest pools; capture cannot reroll a replacement that night. At dawn they disappear. Attackers seek visible, nearby, living explorers in matching land/water, with a 1.1-second warning before an eight-stud strike. They share a 2.5-second per-victim damage cooldown, retain wall/ground checks and cannot attack while restrained. Boars deal 18 health/12 points, panthers 28/20, sharks 30/24; point loss clamps at zero. Ark/spawn and checkpoint radii, fresh-character grace, ForceFields and occupied seats protect explorers. Death preserves inventory/gear and uses the existing checkpoint respawn.

Flashlight cells buy 120 seconds of active beam time. Switching off pauses battery use until respawn; unused cells persist. Permanent night vision toggles a local tint/nearby light at night without battery drain. All equipment state and consumption are server-controlled; lighting is personal client presentation. The reward chime is a short ascending pair of Roblox bundled bell tones in its own SoundGroup. Only authoritative capture effects and positive chest results trigger it, once per chest result even when skipped. It grants no rewards and stays independent of music muting.
