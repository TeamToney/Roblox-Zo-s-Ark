# Verify Zo's Ark

## Automated checks

Run `bash scripts/bootstrap.sh` once on Linux x86_64, then `bash scripts/check.sh` after a change. The test runner exits unsuccessfully if any check fails. It reports the number of actual domain cases, strictly analyzes shared modules, compiles every game script, builds the place, and verifies embedded script counts and source content.

The economy suite covers exact capture awards, bait use, full packs, gear gates, shop debits, repeated purchases, invalid trade quantities, atomic sanctuary transfers, travel limits and milestone boundaries, daily resets, duplicate reward claims, event boundaries and quotas, saved-profile sanitization, and a 1,000-action mixed session. The profile-store suite injects a fake service to test session conflicts, lease expiry, stale writers, release/reconnect, corrupted records, unsupported schemas, transient and exhausted service errors, failed saves, and temporary Studio data isolation. These are Luau tests, not engine simulations or live service verification.

Movement cases cover separate ten-step/stroke boundaries, partial strides, multiple thresholds, rejected modes/speeds/intervals, additive old-save defaults, reconnects and coexistence with distance milestones. Swim-rule cases cover idle fatigue, partial-tick exhaustion damage, equipment combinations, land/boat recovery, airborne non-recovery, flotation expiry and limits, first aid, malformed elapsed time and fresh respawn reserves. Animal-blueprint cases validate every species' geometry, animation references, part budget, ground clearance and distinguishing anatomy. These checks do not run Terrain sampling, Humanoid physics, replication or a rendered Studio model.

The map suite checks image orientation, doorway gaps, the atrium polygon, aligned nursery access, disjoint foundation tiling around water, habitat surface types and dock transitions. A structural raster check expands a three-stud-wide player against the builder's wall data and confirms every room center is connected to the entrance. It checks a conservative boat route through the sea gate. These planar checks exclude decorative props and do not replace Roblox collision, streaming or device testing. `docs/map-1-layout.svg` is regenerated from the same Luau data.

The archipelago checker adds both new island traces: footprint separation, water/ground habitats, structural access from each landing to every room/checkpoint/chest/shore exit, and conservative sea routes between all three islands. Exploration tests cover one-time checkpoint rewards, respawn IDs, old-record defaults, regional capture progress, expedition claims, chest probabilities, daily/reconnect limits, independent players, full packs, owned equipment, point/death traps, and exclusion of event or unhidden Rare+ prizes. Wildlife tests verify visible versus obscure pools, event habitat placement and same-island room eligibility. Presentation regressions cover stale store replies, closing while an inspection is pending, cutscene suspension, escape behavior for every species, finite steering when standing directly on an animal, blocked/cramped escape probes, bounded cutscene timelines/effect budgets, server prize titles and snared misses. They do not simulate GUI hit testing, prompt exploits, Roblox PathfindingService, rendered cutscenes, Humanoid death or actual respawning.

## Studio checklist — still requires execution

Open the generated place in Studio and use a fresh Play session. The UI should say `SESSION`; the world is created when the server starts. Check Server and Client Output for errors. Test an unpublished local file with Studio API access disabled: temporary journals must start without even acquiring a DataStore. The injected service tests cover this isolation; the real Studio startup still requires verification.

- Spawn and respawn safely in the southern entrance, including the first Play session when characters can load before procedural world creation. Reach Meadow Courtyard, the lower gallery and every habitat. Walk around the pointed Atrium and enter its north doorway to reach Ark Outfitters. Check wall/door collision and camera obstruction.
- Close/reopen every panel, scroll the journal/store, resize the window, test phone and tablet emulation, and verify touch prompts. Check keyboard, controller selection, movement, and UI navigation with an actual controller before claiming controller support.
- Catch a rabbit, observe the stipend and Ark Points, and verify that the journal count increases. Wait for an occasional failure and verify no award. Tier 1 gear must reject deer and rare animals with a helpful message. Wildlife should stay within its habitat and avoid walls; prompts and server checks must prevent captures through walls.
- At the storefront, trade one animal; its discovery must remain. Buying an upgrade must debit its listed price exactly once. Away from the storefront, buy and trade requests must fail. Rare sanctuary transfers require a second confirmation click.
- Verify Ranger Net, snorkel, Expedition Net, rescue tranquilizer, Deepwater Kit, pack upgrade, and bait. Gear is automatic; bait should be consumed on an eligible failed attempt too.
- Descend Tidewater Hall's boarding steps. Pilot a boat forward, reverse and turn through the eastern sea gate; jump out, use the swim steps, board again and try all passenger seats. Check lagoon walls, shore/dock collision and its 45-second unoccupied return. An unseated player must not control a boat; disconnecting must not leave one moving.
- Reach fish/turtles in Sunlit Reef Pool and dolphins/whales in Bluewater Pool and the Open Sea extension. Check prompts while swimming and seated. Large animals must fit their zones and bodies should stay near the water surface as streaming loads/unloads them.
- Attempt to swim beneath each outside island edge, under the campus and along lagoon walls. Solid foundation must meet the seabed with no passage below it; boats must still leave through the sea gate. Swim onto all four marked shore stairways and the existing marine steps, then walk back onto land.
- With no gear, remain in water both moving and idle: endurance should reach zero after 30 seconds and then drain 10 health/second. Verify the warning at 10 seconds, health display, death/respawn reset, land/boat recovery, and that jumping or seat spam does not grant step/stroke bonuses. Test snorkel (60s), jacket (90s alone/120s with snorkel), diving (180s) and diving+jacket (240s), on R6 and R15 characters.
- Use a life ring at low endurance: restore 15 seconds, wear a visible ring and pause fatigue for 20 seconds. First aid must heal at most MaxHealth and restore 20 seconds. Two clients racing the same pickup must consume it once; remote/out-of-range, through-wall, dead and seated activations must fail. After 90 seconds, the pickup must reappear in nearby water and remain reachable under streaming.
- Walk ten estimated steps (30 studs): receive exactly 2 coins/1 point. Swim ten strokes (40 studs): receive 3 coins/2 points. Verify separate HUD counters, fractional progress and reconnects, no reward while idle/airborne/seated/teleporting, and continued 500-stud boat milestones. Counters estimate gait from movement; they do not measure animation keyframes.
- Inspect all 27 animals from several angles and during movement: birds/peacock fan, rabbit ears, fox tail, deer antlers, goat/ibex horns, monkey tail, cat patterns, fish/shark vertical tails, mammal flukes, seal whiskers, axolotl gills, turtle shell and manta wings. Check that animation and the wider collision-clearance envelope keep animals out of walls. Profile the extra model parts and Terrain sampling on actual devices.
- Sail northeast to Canopy Reach and southeast to Moonstone Isle. Walk every route from each landing, use all seven boats, swim onto each pier's side steps and each outer shore exit. Verify no passage beneath either new island, no missing floor tiles, water in the coves, and no boat collision on the conservative sea corridors. Confirm the three-island chart, player dot, CP discovery/active colors and chart resizing under streaming.
- Activate all seven checkpoints. First activations must award 15 coins/10 points once; revisits only change respawn. Reset/die at each island, rejoin a staging server, and confirm the saved active checkpoint is used safely. An unvisited checkpoint ID must not activate through a forged request. Verify island capture totals, one Rare+ Moonstone requirement and all four expedition rewards/duplicate claims.
- Hold Rescue for 0.65–1.2 seconds by rarity and verify 2.25-second server pacing. Observe Common/Uncommon land animals travel through connected doorways to other rooms on their own island. Confirm wall sweeps stop blocked paths, no teleports occur on failed navigation, path computations remain bounded, and population counts do not multiply after animals leave their home room. Rare/Epic/Legendary wildlife must be possible in visible and hidden habitats on every island and sea route, with higher budgets on paid islands. Rare wildlife must remain near its spawn habitat. Check the hidden coral entrance and event mantas there.
- Open all 12 chests with two test clients. Each client gets one independent opening per chest/UTC day; repeat/spam/reconnect attempts must not reroll a result. Use a staging-only outcome harness to exercise coins, animals, permanent gear, full-pack conversion, all-owned gear fallback, point penalties down to zero and lethal traps. Verify death returns to the active checkpoint with inventory/gear intact. Out-of-range and through-wall prompt activations must fail. Check lid animation and that the trap warning remains visible. Test midnight UTC rollover without changing the host clock.
- Travel 500 horizontal studs at normal speed. Verify the 25-coin/15-point reward once per boundary; a large teleport or respawn must not count. This is a plausibility filter, not complete movement anti-cheat.
- With Studio's default Aurora preview, catch three mantas with the required gear, claim the prize once, verify the title and personal three-catch quota. Set `ZoPreviewEvent=false` on Workspace to test real timing. Live windows start at each Unix half-hour and last eight minutes. Confirm start/end spawn cleanup, quota reset next tide, and no reward from past-tide progress.
- Complete each daily challenge and claim its reward once. Verify UTC day rollover with a staging test harness; do not alter the production host clock.
