ZO'S ARK - OPEN THIS GAME IN ROBLOX STUDIO

1. Save ZosArk-ThreeIslands.rbxl onto your Windows PC using Download raw file on GitHub.
2. In Studio, stop the current test with the red Stop button.
3. In Studio press Ctrl+O (File > Open from File).
4. Select ZosArk-ThreeIslands.rbxl from your Downloads folder (or wherever you saved it).
5. Press F5 / Play.

The edit-mode scene can look empty because the world is built when Play starts.
The game should show the ZO'S ARK interface and the southern entrance.
A grassland scene with Roblox's WELCOME sign is the starter place, not this game.

THREE-ISLAND EXPEDITION UPDATE
The chart now shows Canopy Reach northeast and Moonstone Isle southeast.
There are 27 animal species, 7 checkpoint respawns and 12 daily surprise chests.
Chests are free but may remove points or kill your character. Checkpoints help!
Hold Rescue for 0.65-1.2 seconds by rarity. Hidden rooms/coves improve rare odds.

WATER SURVIVAL
Look for the SWIM ENDURANCE / HP bar and step/stroke counters above navigation.
Swimming starts with 30 seconds of endurance. Visit the store for water gear.
Orange life rings and green first-aid floats help you stay in the water longer.
The chart's four EXIT markers lead to shore stairs. Animal models are updated.

Studio uses temporary test data by default. Nothing is published by opening it.

OUTFITTERS & WILDLIFE CHASE UPDATE
Twelve species now run or briefly fly away. Birds land again after a short burst.
Ark Outfitters is a walk-in storefront with shelves, models and price tags.
Store shows 3D merchandise previews and coin / Ark Point purchase buttons.
Field Kit: activate sprint boosts, place gentle snares, scan nearby wildlife,
or equip your Safari Hat / Aurora Footsteps cosmetic trail.
Keyboard: K opens Field Kit; 1 sprint, 2 snare, 3 scanner.
Sprints last 45 seconds; unused charges can be bought or found in chests.
Chests celebrate prizes with confetti, balloons and increasingly big fireworks.
Robux bundles say Coming soon until the creator adds Developer Product IDs.
Setup instructions are in docs/ROBUX_SETUP.md in the source repository.

ISLAND PASSPORT & ANIMATED WALLET UPDATE
The coin / Ark Point cards stay on screen, with count-up and gain/spend animations.
Open TRAVEL (T) to buy permanent Canopy or Moonstone island access.
Canopy: 1,200 coins / 800 points. Moonstone: 2,600 coins / 1,700 points.
Once unlocked, sail for free or pay for an optional one-way teleport.
Teleport fees: Ark 25 coins/15 points; Canopy 65/40; Moonstone 90/60.
One Teleport Pass can pay for any owned destination. Entry is sold separately.
Click a shelf item or price tag to select it, then choose your purchase currency.
Rare, Epic and Legendary wildlife can spawn on all maps and sea routes.
