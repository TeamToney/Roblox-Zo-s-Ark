ZO'S ARK - OPEN THIS GAME IN ROBLOX STUDIO

1. Save ZosArk-ThreeIslands.rbxl onto your Windows PC using Download raw file on GitHub.
2. In Studio, stop the current test with the red Stop button.
3. In Studio press Ctrl+O (File > Open from File).
4. Select ZosArk-ThreeIslands.rbxl from your Downloads folder (or wherever you saved it).
5. Press F5 / Play.

The edit-mode scene can look empty because the world is built when Play starts.
The game should show the ZO'S ARK interface and the southern entrance.
A grassland scene with Roblox's WELCOME sign is the starter place, not this game.

THREE-ISLAND EXPEDITION UPDATE
The chart now shows Canopy Reach northeast and Moonstone Isle southeast.
There are 30 animal species, 7 checkpoint respawns and 12 daily surprise chests.
Chests are free but may remove points or kill your character. Checkpoints help!
Hold Rescue for 0.65-1.2 seconds by rarity. Hidden rooms/coves improve rare odds.

WATER SURVIVAL
Look for the SWIM ENDURANCE / HP bar and step/stroke counters above navigation.
Swimming starts with 30 seconds of endurance. Visit the store for water gear.
Orange life rings and green first-aid floats help you stay in the water longer.
The chart's four EXIT markers lead to shore stairs. Animal models are updated.

Studio uses temporary test data by default. Nothing is published by opening it.

OUTFITTERS & WILDLIFE CHASE UPDATE
All species run, swim or briefly fly away after a miss; twelve also flee on approach.
Birds land again after a four-second burst. Gentle snares hold animals still.
Ark Outfitters is a walk-in storefront with shelves, models and price tags.
Store shows 3D merchandise previews and coin / Ark Point purchase buttons.
Field Kit: activate sprint boosts, place gentle snares, scan nearby wildlife,
or equip your Safari Hat / Aurora Footsteps cosmetic trail.
Keyboard: K opens Field Kit; 1 sprint, 2 snare, 3 scanner.
Sprints last 45 seconds; unused charges can be bought or found in chests.
Chests open in a fullscreen 3D cutscene with a moving camera and prize reveal.
Better rewards add balloons and bigger fireworks. Skip: button, Space, Esc or Back.
Skipping keeps the server-awarded prize. Trap results have a danger reveal.
Robux bundles say Coming soon until the creator adds Developer Product IDs.
Setup instructions are in docs/ROBUX_SETUP.md in the source repository.

ISLAND PASSPORT & ANIMATED WALLET UPDATE
The coin / Ark Point cards stay on screen, with count-up and gain/spend animations.
Open TRAVEL (T) to buy permanent Canopy or Moonstone island access.
Canopy: 1,200 coins / 800 points. Moonstone: 2,600 coins / 1,700 points.
Once unlocked, sail for free or pay for an optional one-way teleport.
Teleport fees: Ark 25 coins/15 points; Canopy 65/40; Moonstone 90/60.
One Teleport Pass can pay for any owned destination. Entry is sold separately.
Click a shelf item or price tag to select it, then choose your purchase currency.
Close with X, Esc, controller Back or a click outside the panel, even while loading.
Rare, Epic and Legendary wildlife can spawn on all maps and sea routes.
Paid islands and hidden habitats have better odds. Colors are more vibrant.
Robux map unlocks and 5-pass packs are prepared, awaiting creator Product IDs.

ARK RADIO - BACKGROUND MUSIC
Open Music in the navigation or press N.
Choose Lofi, Hip Hop, Pop, Club, Trap or Ambient.
Select a track, use Prev/Next, and adjust your personal volume with +/-.
Music starts on Lofi at 25%. Use Music ON/OFF or the banner's music button.
The playlist advances and repeats; missing tracks are skipped automatically.
Music lowers during chest cutscenes. World sounds have separate volume.
Tracks come from Roblox's music library; a first search can take a few seconds.
If a playlist cannot load, try another style or use Retry after a short wait.
Your choices last through respawns and reset on a new session.

NIGHT EXPEDITIONS & CASH-REGISTER CHIMES
Successful captures and positive chest finds now ring with a short chi-ching.
The sky cycles through 20 minutes of day and 5 minutes of night.
A fresh server begins at dawn; its first night arrives after a full 20 minutes.
Night captures give 2x capture coins/points; dangerous predators give 3x.
Boars, panthers and sharks appear at night and can take points and health.
Rest safely near the Ark or checkpoints. Dawn removes night predators.
Buy night gear from Outfitters using coins or points, then open Field Kit (K):
F - flashlight: one purchased cell gives 120 seconds; switch off to save battery.
V - night vision: permanent upgrade, works after dark without battery use.
4 - tranquilizer: sedate the nearest visible predator for 45 seconds.
5 - predator snare: a reinforced land trap with 3-minute wait/hold timers.
Predators must be sedated or held by the reinforced snare before capture.
Normal rescue-tier, water-gear and pack limits still apply. Use darts for sharks.
All actions also have Field Kit buttons for touch/controller.

MOVING TREASURE & WILDLIFE
Eight chests and four wrapped surprise gifts start in random suitable locations.
They move again about every five minutes, or shortly after opening, once everyone
is at least 70 studs away. Moving never resets a find's daily opening limit.
Your opened finds show OPENED TODAY until midnight UTC.
Animals choose new compatible habitats when spawning and roam more frequently.
Rarity, island access, hidden habitats and event locations keep their rules.

TRAIL LEAP & WILDLIFE IDENTIFICATION
Walls are now fourteen studs tall; normal jumps reach five studs. Use doorways.
Buy Trail Leap at Outfitters for 110 coins or 75 points, then press 6 / Field Kit.
It gives sixteen-stud jumps for 45 seconds and ends on respawn.
Animal names now appear only within twelve studs in clear view.
Learn silhouettes and coat patterns from the Journal's new 3D portraits.
Visible predators give a generic warning within twenty-four studs.
