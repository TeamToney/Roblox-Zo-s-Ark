# Zo's Ark

Explore an Ark sanctuary campus, outdoor habitats and the surrounding sea. Collect wildlife with nonlethal rescue gear and grow a field journal. Return to the Ark Atrium to trade animals into the sanctuary, buy equipment, and prepare for rarer encounters.

This playable prototype adapts all **three uploaded floor plans** into the Ark Sanctuary, Canopy Reach and Moonstone Isle, combining their building layouts with outdoor biomes and open-sea exploration. It includes 30 animal species, rarity and equipment gates, a coin/Ark Point economy, a collection journal, sanctuary trading, a gear store, boats, daily and island challenges, checkpoint respawns, surprise chests, Aurora Tide events, and a session-locked saving adapter. Architecture and animals use original procedural art. Player-to-player trading is not implemented. Guaranteed Robux bundles have receipt handling, but remain unavailable until the experience owner configures Developer Products; see [Robux setup](docs/ROBUX_SETUP.md).

The water-survival update adds solid island foundations down to the seabed, four marked shore stairways, swim endurance, recovery pickups, and bonuses for walking and swimming. Animals now have species-specific silhouettes, coat patterns, shells, fins and feathers, with animated limbs, wings and tails made from original Roblox primitives.

See the [colored layout diagram](docs/map-1-layout.svg) and [Map 1 design notes](docs/MAP1_DESIGN.md). The map preserves the southern entrance, lower gallery corridor, pointed central atrium, flanking rooms and large eastern hall; smaller partitions are simplified. The scale is chosen for play, not recovered from the scan's measurements.

The [three-island overview](docs/archipelago-layout.svg) and [island design notes](docs/ISLANDS_DESIGN.md) explain the two additional traces. Canopy Reach is northeast of the Ark; Moonstone Isle is southeast. Each new island has three checkpoints, four surprise chests, wildlife and an expedition challenge. Their permanent entry passes are purchased in **Travel**; the Ark and shared seas remain free. Seven boats serve the archipelago. The main campus remains in its original position and orientation.

## Open and play

**Windows download:** open [ZosArk-ThreeIslands.rbxl](downloads/ZosArk-ThreeIslands.rbxl) on GitHub and use **Download raw file**. This ready-to-open place is a snapshot of the source in this branch; no build tools are needed to try it. Stop any current Studio playtest, use **File → Open from File** (or **Ctrl+O**), select the downloaded file, and press **F5**. The map appears when Play starts. See [the opening instructions](downloads/OPEN_IN_STUDIO.txt). The earlier Map 1 download path remains an alias for the current game.

For development builds:

1. Install [Roblox Studio](https://create.roblox.com/docs/studio/setup) on Windows or macOS.
2. Open `build/ZosArk.rbxlx` in Studio. The build is generated by the commands below and is not checked into Git.
3. Press **Play**. The server generates the world at runtime; the edit-mode place will initially look empty.
4. Start in the southern entrance lobby. Close the guide with **×** and visit Meadow Courtyard west of the central avenue. Approach a rabbit or fox and hold **Rescue animal**; on touch devices, tap and hold its prompt.
5. Follow the avenue north and go around the pointed Ark Atrium to its north entrance. Visit Ark Outfitters inside. Open **Journal** to trade animals or **Store** to buy permanent equipment upgrades.
6. Visit Tidewater Hall in the northeast. Descend its southern boarding steps, borrow a boat, and use normal movement controls to steer; jump to leave. Sail through the eastern sea gate to find more marine wildlife. Each boat seats three players including the pilot.

Use the bottom navigation on all devices. Desktop shortcuts: **J** journal, **B** store, **C** challenges, **M** chart, **K** field kit, **T** travel, **N** music, **H** guide; **1** sprint, **2** snare, **3** scanner. Field Kit buttons provide the same actions on touch devices. Boats are free and return to their dock after 45 seconds without occupants. Gear upgrades apply automatically, including the nonlethal tranquilizer upgrade; there is no aiming or weapon combat system yet.

### Island expeditions and surprise chests

- Hold **Rescue animal** for **0.65–1.2 seconds** depending on rarity; the server capture cooldown is **2.25 seconds**. Common/Uncommon land animals can follow navigable doorways between rooms on their home island. Failed paths stop movement; wildlife never teleports through walls.
- **Rare, Epic and Legendary wildlife** can spawn on every island and sea route in matching habitats. They are scarce on the Ark; paid islands have higher rates, and secluded habitats multiply those rates by 1.8. Wild rare animals stay near their home habitat. Mythic Aurora Mantas remain exclusive to the timed coral-alcove event.
- Activate any of the **7 checkpoints** to set your respawn; the first activation awards **15 coins + 10 points**. The active checkpoint persists with the journal. Both new islands have a landing checkpoint, an interior checkpoint and a hidden checkpoint. Check **Challenges** for four one-time expedition prizes.
- The **12 surprise chests** are free, with one opening per explorer per chest per UTC day. Defaults: **35% coins**, **30% an animal**, **16% equipment/cosmetics**, **2.8% sprint boost**, **1.2% snare**, **10% a point-loss trap**, **5% a death trap**. Only obscure chests can reveal Rare+ animals; event mantas are excluded. Functional equipment is permanent and automatically active; equip cosmetics through Field Kit. An animal prize with a full pack converts to its coin value and discovery points.
- Point traps remove 12% of current Ark Points, at least 10 when available, clamped at zero. Death traps kill the character and respawn at the selected checkpoint; existing gear, animals and discoveries remain. Reopening the same chest that day cannot reroll the result. New openings become available at midnight UTC.

Treasure locations vary each server: eight wooden chests and four wrapped surprise gifts share the twelve existing daily claim IDs. Each find tries a new suitable room or secluded water habitat about every five minutes, or shortly after opening, once everyone is at least 70 studs away. Finds never move during their lid animation. Moving does not grant another opening; your claimed finds show **OPENED TODAY** until midnight UTC. Hidden finds stay hidden, water finds stay in water, and island access still applies.

Animals also choose different compatible habitats when they spawn, including additional outdoor nature rooms. They retain their original island, biome, concealment and sea-route rules, along with the same population and rarity budgets. Living animals walk, swim or fly between their usual roaming pauses; they do not teleport to reshuffle. Event mantas keep their special event site.

### Outfitters, escapes and field kit

Ark Outfitters is a walk-in shop in the existing pointed Atrium, with an open north-facing doorway, striped awning, counter, shelving, modeled merchandise and visible prices. The Store panel adds category filters, 3D item previews, and separate coin/Ark Point purchase buttons. All fifteen items support either currency. Spending points also affects the existing level formula.

Every species flees after a missed eligible rescue, including rabbits and marine animals. Twelve skittish species also flee on close approach: fox, deer, raccoon, boar, parrot, eagle, jaguar, tiger, snow leopard, frostlight fox, ibex and peacock. Four-second bursts steer away from the pursuing player and try shorter sidesteps around obstacles; birds rise briefly and land. Standing on an animal cannot block its navigation. Gentle snares intentionally hold animals still. Fleeing lowers capture chance by 14 percentage points. Capture holds and recovery are checked on the server. Ground/water checks and wall sweeps constrain movement; rare and secluded animals stay in their home habitats.

New purchases:

| Item | Coins | Ark Points | Effect |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Expedition Sprint | 90 | 60 | One 45-second run/swim boost; no stacking |
| Gentle Animal Snare | 120 | 80 | One 45-second land trap; holds one animal for 8 seconds, +22 percentage points capture chance |
| Wildlife Scanner | 850 | 550 | Permanent equipment; 20-second scan within 140 studs, 30-second cooldown |
| Ranger Camouflage | 550 | 360 | Permanent equipment; reduces animal alert radius to 55% |
| Safari Explorer Hat | 240 | 160 | Equippable cosmetic |
| Aurora Footsteps | 380 | 250 | Equippable cosmetic trail |

Field Kit activates supplies anywhere; placing snares requires level dry ground, consumes a charge only on a valid placement and permits two active snares per player. Gear/tier/full-pack rules still apply to snared rescues. Scanner signals last only during the scan and show at most six nearby creatures. Unused supplies and equipped cosmetics persist in the v1 journal; active sprint/scan timers are session state. Sprint raises running speed from 18 to 28 studs/second; swimming also accelerates without extending endurance. Server movement sampling accounts for the boost when awarding ten-step/stroke bonuses.

Chest openings play a short fullscreen 3D cutscene: a camera moves toward a shaking chest, its lid opens, and the actual prize floats into view. Better prizes add balloons, confetti and larger firework displays. Reward scenes last 4.4–6.5 seconds; trap reveals last 3.8 seconds with a distinct danger effect. Skip with the on-screen button, Space, Escape or controller Back. The server awards/reserves the result before playback, so skipping never loses or rerolls it. Completion, skipping, respawn and errors restore input and remove the scene. The paid shop offers only guaranteed bundles; opening surprise chests remains free.

### Island access, teleports and the fixed wallet

The coin and Ark Point cards stay in the screen header when panels close and after respawning. Balances count smoothly to each authoritative update; earned/spent deltas float and fade with a brief icon pulse. Bright turquoise water, saturated biome floors, warmer paths and clearer rarity colors make the world more vibrant.

Open **Travel** or press **T**. Ark Sanctuary and the shared sea are free. Canopy Reach costs **1,200 coins or 800 points**; Moonstone Isle costs **2,600 coins or 1,700 points**. These are permanent island unlocks, separate from optional teleport fees. The server checks physical entry, captures, checkpoints, chests, scanners and respawns. Older visited checkpoints and catches are preserved, but a prior visit is not a purchased entry pass.

| Teleport destination | Coins per trip | Points per trip | Alternative |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Ark landing | 25 | 15 | 1 Teleport Pass |
| Canopy landing | 65 | 40 | 1 Teleport Pass |
| Moonstone landing | 90 | 60 | 1 Teleport Pass |

Teleports require an unlocked destination and a living, unseated explorer. There is a 6-second cooldown; invalid, already-at-landing or failed moves do not charge. Sailing is free after unlocking. Outfitters sells single passes for 80 coins/50 points, and the prepared Robux collection offers 5 passes or permanent map unlocks. Robux prices/IDs still need creator configuration.

Base per-spawn rarity budgets, when matching species exist: Ark **1.5% Rare / 0.5% Epic / 0.08% Legendary**; Canopy **5% / 1.8% / 0.4%**; Moonstone **7.5% / 3% / 0.8%**. Hidden habitats multiply them by 1.8 and open seaways by 1.2. Missing rarity budgets return to common wildlife. These are spawn probabilities, not guarantees or capture odds. Five added sea routes target 15 additional animals; the new Legendary Sunburst Ray can appear in reefs and seaways. The archipelago targets 96 ordinary animals plus 2 event mantas.

Click/tap a shelf item or its price tag to open that selected product. Store preview models are selectable too. The purchase card offers coins or points; it does not charge merely for selecting an item. Close any notebook panel with ×, Escape, controller Back or a click outside it. Closing works locally while a server request is pending, and a late inspection response cannot reopen it.

### Day, night and dangerous wildlife

A full cycle lasts **25 real minutes: 20 minutes of day, 5 minutes of night**. Fresh servers start at dawn, giving a full 20 minutes before the first night. The banner shows world time and the next dawn/nightfall. Lighting changes gradually. Every successful animal capture plays a two-note cash-register-style chime; positive chest finds ring at their cutscene reveal, or once when skipped. Trap chests and failed attempts stay silent. Music muting does not mute reward sounds.

Night captures award **2× the normal capture coins and Ark Points**. Dangerous night predators pay **3× their base capture rewards**. This affects the catch stipend/discovery points; sanctuary sale prices, chest prizes and challenge rewards retain their normal values. Each catch counts as one animal for quests and event quotas.

Moonlit Tusk Boars, Midnight Panthers and Nightfall Reef Sharks emerge from nine population slots, three per island, in compatible habitats. Each slot can spawn one predator per night, with no replacement until the next night; dawn removes survivors. Their red danger labels identify them. They pursue nearby explorers, warn for 1.1 seconds before striking, and remove health plus 12/20/24 points per hit. Damage can kill; points never go below zero. Spawn/Ark areas and checkpoints are safe resting places; new characters get 15 seconds of protection. Walls block attacks, seated explorers are safe, and sharks target swimmers. Predators stay near their chosen habitat.

Buy night equipment at Outfitters with either currency:

| Equipment | Coins | Ark Points | Use |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Field Flashlight Cell | 75 | 50 | **F** / Field Kit; 120 seconds of beam time, pause by switching off |
| Night Vision Goggles | 950 | 620 | **V** / Field Kit; permanent skill, active after dark, no battery |
| Predator Rescue Snare | 180 | 120 | **5** / Field Kit; one ground trap, waits 3 minutes and holds one land predator up to 3 minutes |
| Rescue Tranquilizer | 1,800 | 1,170 | Existing Tier 4 upgrade; **4** / Field Kit sedates the nearest visible predator within 35 studs for 45 seconds, 12-second cooldown |

Predators **must actually be sedated or caught in a Predator Rescue Snare before capture**. Merely owning a tranquilizer, or using a Gentle Animal Snare, does not qualify. Normal rescue-tier, water-gear and pack-capacity requirements still apply. Use the tranquilizer for sharks; reinforced traps are land-only. Unused cells/snares and night-vision ownership persist; current flashlight charge and toggles reset at respawn. See [night testing](docs/TESTING.md) for the optional Studio preview and unexecuted engine checks.

### Outdoor forests

The default archipelago has 421 trees: 160 around the Ark, 106 on Canopy Reach and 155 on Moonstone Isle. Broadleaf and birch groves surround the Ark, tropical crowns and palms fill Canopy habitats, and cooler pines/birches characterize Moonstone. Each island also has 180 low plants or props: ferns, wildflowers, mossy stones and fallen logs. Sizes, canopy shapes and greens vary while retaining Roblox-style primitive models.

Planting follows dry ground and keeps paved routes, indoor rooms, shore steps, docks, checkpoints, chests, the spawn and Outfitters clear. Habitat centers and doorway-to-center lanes stay open for animal chases. Vegetation is decorative: it has no collision, touch or query surfaces that can block wildlife navigation or capture checks. Models stream individually and have no per-frame animation loop. Visual framing, streaming and mobile performance still need Studio review.

### Ark Radio background music

Open **Music** in the bottom navigation or press **N**. Choose **Lofi, Hip Hop, Pop, Club, Trap or Ambient**, then pick a track or use Previous/Next. Tracks advance and repeat through the playlist automatically. Use **Music ON/OFF**, the quick **♫ ON/OFF** button beside the tide banner, and the volume buttons. Music starts on Lofi at 25% volume. Muting affects only your background music; world sounds and other players keep their own settings. Choices survive character respawns within the session; they reset on rejoining.

Each style loads up to 12 tracks through Roblox's music-library search. The server shares cached results and rate-limits searches; changing radio settings never blocks shop/capture requests or changes progression. Unavailable audio is skipped with bounded load timeouts; a failed playlist offers another style or Retry. Music fades between tracks and lowers during chest cutscenes. Live library availability, track suitability and audible playback still require Studio review. See [music maintenance](docs/MUSIC.md) to pin a reviewed playlist instead of dynamic search.

### Swimming and movement bonuses

- Swim endurance starts at **30 seconds**. A **Reef Snorkel** raises it to **60 seconds**, a **Deepwater Kit** to **180 seconds**, and an **Expedition Life Jacket** adds **60 seconds** to your current water gear. Water gear applies automatically.
- Endurance runs down while in water, even when idle. At zero, exhaustion removes **10 health per second**. Rest on solid land or sit in a boat to recover **8 seconds of endurance per second**. Jumping does not refill it.
- Hold the prompt on an orange floating **life ring** to recover **15 seconds** and receive **20 seconds of flotation**, during which endurance does not drain. Green **first-aid floats** restore **25 health** and **20 seconds** of endurance. Pickups are scattered through the pools, open sea and near shore exits; used pickups return in a new nearby position after **90 seconds**.
- Every **10 walking steps** earns **2 coins + 1 Ark Point**. Every **10 swimming strokes** earns **3 coins + 2 Ark Points**. Steps and strokes are estimated from server-observed movement (3 studs per step, 4 per stroke); boats and airtime do not count. Partial progress persists in existing journals. The 500-stud exploration bonus remains available, including by boat.
- Use the four **EXIT** markers on the chart to find the outer shore stairways. The marine hall's boarding and swimming steps remain open.

## Build and verify in the cloud / Linux

Requires Python 3, curl, and Linux x86_64. No API keys or Roblox credentials are needed for CLI development.

```bash
bash scripts/bootstrap.sh
bash scripts/check.sh
```

The bootstrap downloads pinned Rojo 7.7.1 and Luau 0.742 archives, verifies the published upstream SHA-256 checksums in `tools.json`, and extracts only the named executables into ignored `.tools/`. Checks run strict analysis, deterministic game/map/save-adapter tests, structural map reachability and boat-route checks, compilation of every game script, and a Rojo build. They also regenerate `docs/map-1-layout.svg` from the same layout data used by the game. The resulting place is `build/ZosArk.rbxlx`. CI performs the same checks and retains a place artifact.

To refresh the checked-in Studio downloads after validating source changes, run `python3 scripts/package_studio.py` and commit both updated places with their source. Packaging rebuilds the binary/XML places, refreshes the legacy download alias and verifies the ZIP archive. CI runs this after the checks.

CLI checks do not run the Roblox engine. [Studio checks](docs/TESTING.md) cover character movement, prompts, seats, visual layout, replication, live DataStore integration, and performance. This Linux cloud session cannot open or connect to a local Studio.

## Edit with Rojo on your computer

Install the matching [Rojo CLI](https://github.com/rojo-rbx/rojo/releases/tag/v7.7.1) for your operating system and the matching official **Rojo 7.7.1** Studio plugin (`Rojo.rbxm` from that release, installed as a local plugin). The Linux bootstrap is not a Mac or Windows installer.

From a local copy of this repository on the same computer as Studio:

```bash
rojo serve default.project.json
```

Open the place, connect the Rojo plugin to the local server, and sync the source. Do not expect the Studio plugin on your computer to reach a localhost server running in this cloud machine. With Studio open locally, the ChatGPT desktop app or Codex can also connect through Studio's own MCP server for iterative building.

## Save modes

- Studio uses **session-only data by default** and labels the HUD `SESSION`. Stopping the test resets it. Studio also previews Aurora Tide by default.
- Published servers save profiles in `ZosArk_Profiles_v1`. They acquire a renewable session lease with `UpdateAsync`, autosave every 60 seconds, and release the lease on exit/shutdown. A failed load or conflicting lease prevents entry rather than replacing a journal with blank data.
- To test real saving, use a separate staging experience. Enable **Studio Access to API Services**, set Workspace attribute `ZoUseLiveDataInStudio` to `true`, and set `ZoPreviewEvent` to `false`. Never enable Studio API access against production data for casual tests. No live saving has been verified by the cloud CLI checks.
- Server crashes can lose progress since the last successful save. A session lease may take up to four minutes to expire after a hard crash. Keep these limitations in release notes and test recovery before launch.
